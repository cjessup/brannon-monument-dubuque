# Brannon Monument Company — research notes

Live site: https://www.brannonmonument.com/

Confirmed line of business: a family memorial monument company (cemetery memorials and related granite work), not a general contractor or funeral home. The live pages offer family and single monuments, etchings, mausoleums, markers, bronze markers, family markers to match, cremation memorials, pet markers, vases, statuary, porcelain photo medallions, final date inscriptions, monument restoration, granite and stone signage, benches, decorative landscape rocks, bronze plaques, house number engraving, and brick paver fundraising projects. Showrooms are in Dubuque, Marion (described on the directions page as the Cedar Rapids location), and Manchester. Founded in 1926. Sister sites are linked, not rebuilt: https://www.novakandbrannon.com/ and https://www.manchestermonuments.com/.

Brand taken from the live CSS and logo files: green `#0b9444`, charcoal `#1e1f1e`, paper `#f3f3f4`, Roboto Condensed, and the green script wordmarks. Year-count lines on the live site (over 75 years, over 88 years, over 89 years) are left as written. The directions page prints Dubuque as `Dubuque, IA 52001`; the footer on every live page prints `2100 STONE HILL DRIVE, DUBUQUE, IA 52003`. Both are kept where they appear.

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photos, or branding were copied.

### American Monument & Granite Co. — https://americanmonumentandgranite.com/

- Full-width photograph first, with the business name and next step kept clear of the picture. The demo puts the headline under an uncovered photo.
- Portfolio grouped by memorial type (upright, slant, marker, bench, bronze, signs) so a family can jump to a kind of stone. Used for the category filters on the portfolio page.
- A direct phone action and a short contact form near the work, without a pricing table.
- Reviews sit high on the home page as a trust block. Their review content was not reused.

### Quincy Memorials — https://quincymemorials.com/

- Memorial types presented as a tight, scannable set (headstones, markers, cremation, etching) instead of only long columns of body copy. Used for the service chips and the home path list.
- Several offices shown together, each with its own phone, so the right location is obvious. Used for the Dubuque / Marion / Manchester blocks.
- A short established-date line before the longer history. Used as the “Est. 1926” bar.
- The gallery is the proof, with a clear way to talk to a person. Used as the portfolio-plus-call pattern.

### Gifford Monument Works — https://giffordmonument.com/

- More than one region, each with its own phone kept visible. Used so Dubuque, Marion, and Manchester numbers stay on the page rather than behind one generic contact link.
- The path from conversation to an approved design to setting the stone, told in the company’s own words. Brannon already says families approve a computer layout before production; that sentence stays theirs and is not replaced with Gifford’s process or prices.
- Gallery entered by kind of work. Reinforced the portfolio categories already on the live Brannon site (benches, bronze markers, etchings, and the rest).

Not borrowed: Gifford’s package prices, price-match language, and guarantees, and Quincy’s catalog counts, staff pages, and review totals.
